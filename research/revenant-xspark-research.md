# Revenant XSpark: research brief

**Research date:** 2026-07-24
**Primary focus:** BGMI/PUBG Mobile lineage, merger context, pre-merger player performance, and major organization-wide results
**Geography:** India

## Executive summary

Revenant XSpark was announced on **31 October 2024** as a collaboration between Revenant Esports and Team XSpark. The announcement combined Revenant's multi-title infrastructure with Team XSpark's BGMI brand, championship roster, and audience. Tanmay “Sc0utOP” Singh became brand ambassador, while Rohit Jagasia remained the Revenant-side founder/CEO figure. The announcement also named **Sarang, SPRAYGOD, Shadow, and Jokerr**, coached by **Osmium**, as the new organization's BGMI lineup.

The key competitive fact is that the merged BGMI roster came from **Team XSpark**, not Revenant's own last BGMI five. Team XSpark entered the collaboration after winning both official 2024 national LAN championships:

- **BGIS 2024:** 1st, 142 points across 18 finals matches, 100 elimination points and 42 placement points.
- **BMPS 2024:** 1st, 158 points across 18 finals matches, 102 elimination points and 56 placement points.

The two titles used slightly different lineups:

- BGIS: Shadow7, SPRAYGOD, Sarang, NinjaJOD; coach Osmium.
- BMPS: Shadow7, SPRAYGOD, Sarang, Jokerr; coach Osmium.

Revenant Esports arrived with a broader organization-level record. In 2024 it won VALORANT Challengers South Asia Split 2 and represented India at VCT Ascension Pacific. Its BGMI side had won the Upthrust Multiverse Series and placed sixth at the ESL Snapdragon Pro Series early in the year, but its final pre-merger BGMI lineup did not carry into the merged team's starting four.

Sources: [merger announcement](https://www.indiatodaygaming.com/news/story/revenant-esports-and-team-xspark-join-forces-to-form-revenant-xspark-scoutop-revealed-as-brand-ambassador-5667), [Revenant XSpark on Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/pubgmobile/Revenant_XSpark), [BGIS 2024](https://liquipedia.net/pubgmobile/Battlegrounds_Mobile_India_Series/2024), [BMPS 2024](https://liquipedia.net/pubgmobile/Battlegrounds_Mobile_India_Pro_Series/2024).

## Timeline

### Team XSpark lineage

- **2019-09-10:** Sc0utOP, Owais, Ronak, Paritosh, and InYourDream joined the original XSpark lineup.
- **2019-10-18:** Fnatic acquired that roster.
- **2020:** XSpark returned with Destro, Aditya, EvoO, and 420op; it won PMCO Fall 2020 India.
- **2021-2022:** The organization cycled through Mavi, Sc0utOP, GiLL, ClownY, Ultron, VIRU, and others; it won THUG Invitational Season 4 in 2022.
- **2023-06-04:** Aditya, Pukar, SyedOP, Sarang, and Darklord joined.
- **2023-11-10:** SPRAYGOD joined.
- **2024-01-07:** NinjaJOD and coach Osmium joined.
- **2024-02-22:** Shadow7 joined.
- **2024-06-30:** Team XSpark won BGIS 2024.
- **2024-08-19:** NinjaJOD left and Jokerr joined.
- **2024-09-29:** Team XSpark won BMPS 2024.
- **2024-10-31:** Team XSpark and Revenant Esports announced Revenant XSpark.

### Revenant Esports BGMI lineage

- **2021-06-01:** Original listed BGMI roster began with Encore, Paradox, AustinX, and Topdawg.
- **2022-12-31:** The first BGMI roster disbanded.
- **2023-04-19:** SENSEI joined the revived team.
- **2023-06-09:** Fierce and Apollo joined.
- **2023-11-05:** Revenant placed second at Skyesports Championship 5.0; Fierce won MVP.
- **2024-01-12:** AquaNox and Blaze joined.
- **2024-01-17:** Revenant won Upthrust Esports – The Multiverse Series 2024.
- **2024-02-18:** Revenant placed sixth at ESL Snapdragon Pro Series BGMI.
- **2024-03-29:** Punkk joined.
- **2024-06-30:** Fierce, AquaNox, Blaze, and Punkk left.
- **2024-07-15:** Flawk, Saif, and Blaze joined/rejoined around SENSEI.
- **2024-08-19:** KRATOS joined.
- **2024-10-31:** The collaboration was announced, but the old Revenant BGMI roster continued to exist separately until December.
- **2024-12-07:** Blaze, Flawk, KRATOS, SENSEI, and Saif left and Revenant Esports' separate BGMI listing closed.

Source: [Revenant Esports Liquipedia timeline](https://liquipedia.net/pubgmobile/Revenant_Esports).

### Post-merger BGMI roster evolution

- **2024-10-31 merger roster:** Shadow7, SPRAYGOD, Sarang, Jokerr; coach Osmium.
- **2025-04-11:** Jokerr left.
- **2025-05-07:** SPRAYGOD left, then rejoined with NinjaJOD on 2025-05-17.
- **2025-08-15:** The Shadow7/Sarang/NinjaJOD/Rony roster disbanded; Osmium also left.
- **2025-08-17:** Revenant XSpark acquired the Wild Fangs roster: SENSEI, Saif, Tracegod, and Fierce; FurY and coach EXPLICIT also joined.
- **Late 2025 to 2026:** The lineup turned over again.
- **Research-date lineup:** Tracegod, NinjaJOD, Pain09, Proton, Sukuna; coach EXPLICIT.

Source: [Revenant XSpark Liquipedia timeline](https://liquipedia.net/pubgmobile/Revenant_XSpark).

## Championship breakdown

### BGIS 2024

- Final position: 1st.
- Finals score: 142 points in 18 matches.
- Breakdown: 100 elimination points, 42 placement points.
- Average: 7.89 total points per match; 5.56 elimination points per match.
- Match wins: 1.
- Top-five finishes: 6.
- Roster: Shadow7, SPRAYGOD, Sarang, NinjaJOD.
- Player leaderboard markers: Sarang was second in finals eliminations with 29; NinjaJOD was fourth with 27.
- Prize: ₹60 lakh, approximately $71,973 on the Liquipedia conversion used for the event.

Source: [Liquipedia BGIS 2024](https://liquipedia.net/pubgmobile/Battlegrounds_Mobile_India_Series/2024).

### BMPS 2024

- Final position: 1st.
- Finals score: 158 points in 18 matches.
- Breakdown: 102 elimination points, 56 placement points.
- Average: 8.78 total points per match; 5.67 elimination points per match.
- Match wins: 4.
- Top-five finishes: 7.
- Roster: Shadow7, SPRAYGOD, Sarang, Jokerr.
- SPRAYGOD: overall MVP, 98 finishes across league + finals.
- Sarang: finals MVP, 31 finals finishes.
- Jokerr: 31 finals finishes and Finals Survivor award; he also produced back-to-back match MVPs on finals day two, including a reported 1v6 clutch.
- Prize: ₹75 lakh; the Liquipedia converted value is approximately $89,586.

Sources: [Liquipedia BMPS 2024](https://liquipedia.net/pubgmobile/Battlegrounds_Mobile_India_Pro_Series/2024), [GamingOnPhone finals report](https://gamingonphone.com/esports/battlegrounds-mobile-pro-series-bmps-2024-grand-finals-results-and-updates/).

### Revenant at ESL Snapdragon Pro Series 2024

- Final position: 6th.
- Finals score reported by the results coverage: 159 total points in 18 matches, including 74 elimination points and two wins.
- AquaNox: fifth in the Challenge Finals elimination ranking with 27.
- Core roster: SENSEI, Fierce, AquaNox, Blaze.
- Recorded prize: ₹560,000, approximately $6,746.

Sources: [Liquipedia event page](https://liquipedia.net/pubgmobile/ESL/Snapdragon_Pro_Series/2024/BGMI), [event standings report](https://www.gamesnfans.tv/entity-gaming-wins-the-esl-bgmi-pro-series-2024-lan-finals/).

## Organization-wide achievements around the merger

Revenant was more than a BGMI team. Its broader results explain the strategic logic of the collaboration.

### Pre-merger Revenant highlights

- **VALORANT:** 1st at VALORANT Challengers 2024 South Asia Split 2; 7th-8th at VCT Ascension Pacific 2024. The Split 2 win made it the Indian representative at Ascension.
- **Brawl Stars:** 5th-8th at the 2023 World Finals; multiple Indian Monthly Finals wins in 2023.
- **Pokémon UNITE:** represented India internationally before the collaboration; the merged entity became a 2025 ACL India League partner.
- **Honor of Kings:** 13th-16th at the 2024 Honor of Kings Championship.
- **Community IP:** co-organized the ₹22 lakh Titans Rising BGMI event with Gujarat Titans in October 2024.

## Data quality and gaps

- Liquipedia is dynamic. Winnings, current roster, and result counts can change after this research date.
- Some old Team XSpark records are grouped under the modern Revenant XSpark page because Liquipedia treats the BGMI lineage as continuous.
- Team prize figures should not be presented as equal personal payouts.
- The 2024 player earnings ranking covers all teams a player represented during the calendar year.
- Full player combat statistics were available for some official finals, but not for every Indian invitational.
- The exact ownership/legal structure of the collaboration is not public in the reviewed sources. News reports use both “collaboration,” “partnership,” and “merger”; the public brand outcome is clear, but corporate shareholding is not.
- The current BGMI roster is very different from the October 2024 launch roster. Any public-facing page should label rosters by season/date.
